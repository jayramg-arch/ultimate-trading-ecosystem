## Playbook 1: The "Accumulation Origin" Setup (Long)

This is the flagship bullish setup. You are looking to enter exactly when institutions have finished manipulating retail traders and are preparing to markup the price.

### Step 1: The Wyckoff Narrative (Context)
* Wait for a prolonged downtrend to exhaust itself. 
* Look for the indicator to print an **SC (Selling Climax)** on high volume, followed by an **AR (Automatic Rally)**.
* **The Trigger:** Wait for a **Spring**. This is a brief sweep below the SC low that immediately recovers. It is the ultimate trap for retail short-sellers.
* Wait for an **SOS (Sign of Strength)**: A powerful upward thrust confirming the institutions are now driving price higher.

### Step 2: SMC Confirmation & Entry (Execution)
* The SOS move will almost always create a **Bullish Break of Structure (BOS)** or **Change of Character (CHoCH)**.
* Look at the origin of that SOS move. The SMC indicator should draw a **Bullish Order Block (Green Box)** and a **Bullish Fair Value Gap (Teal Box)**.
* **The Entry:** Do *not* buy the top of the SOS! Wait for price to pull back (this is the Wyckoff **LPS - Last Point of Support**). Place your limit buy order exactly at the top of the **Bullish Order Block**.
* **The Stop Loss:** Place your stop loss safely below the Order Block or below the low of the Wyckoff Spring.

### Step 3: Volume Profile Validation (The Kicker)
* Check the Volume Profile for the range. 
* The **POC (Red Line)** should be resting right around the Spring or the Order Block. This means the institutions built their massive position exactly where you are buying.
* Above your entry, you want to see a **Low Volume Node (LVN)** (a valley in the histogram). This means there is no historical resistance, and price will rapidly slice upward through the vacuum.

## Playbook 2: The "Distribution Trap" Setup (Short)

This is the flagship bearish setup. You are looking to short the market right after institutions have finished offloading their shares to late, greedy retail buyers.

### Step 1: The Wyckoff Narrative (Context)
* Look for a mature uptrend that starts to get choppy.
* The indicator will print a **BC (Buying Climax)** on high volume.
* **The Trigger:** Look for an **UT (Upthrust)**. This is a false breakout above the BC high that immediately fails. It traps retail breakout traders.
* Wait for a **SOW (Sign of Weakness)**: A violent drop on high volume that breaks the trading range support.

### Step 2: SMC Confirmation & Entry (Execution)
* The SOW drop will print a **Bearish CHoCH**.
* Look at the origin of that drop. You should see a **Bearish Order Block (Red Box)** and a **Bearish Fair Value Gap (Orange Box)**.
* **The Entry:** Wait for the weak relief rally (the Wyckoff **LPSY - Last Point of Supply**). Short the market exactly when price taps into the **Bearish Order Block**.
* **The Stop Loss:** Place your stop loss safely above the Bearish Order Block or above the UT high.

### Step 3: Volume Profile Validation (The Kicker)
* Your entry should perfectly align with the **Value Area High (VAH)**. You are shorting right as price attempts to leave the accepted value area but gets rejected.
* Below your entry, you should see the POC. Price will rapidly drop toward the POC.

## Trade Management & Take Profit Strategy

1. **Target 1 (Scale Out):** Take partial profits at the **Point of Control (POC)** if you entered from the outer edges (VAH/VAL). The POC acts as a heavy magnet, but price often stalls there.
2. **Target 2 (Structural Exit):** Target opposing unmitigated SMC zones. If you are long, your final target is the nearest unmitigated Bearish Order Block or Bearish FVG.
3. **Target 3 (Liquidity Sweep):** Look for opposing Wyckoff liquidity. If you are long, take profit just above the previous Wyckoff BC or AR highs, as institutions will use that liquidity to take their own profits.
