01What changed since the guide was written

The standalone indicator no longer exists

This module was merged into the context overlay and later ported to Python. The markdown guide describes a version that is gone, and it is wrong in three specific ways plus one large omission.

The old guide saysThe code does
Order blocks found by scanning 50 bars for an impulse move above 1.5× volatilityPivot-anchored, swing length 5. There is no impulse-threshold input and no lookback scan — the block is tied to a confirmed swing
Gap minimum is 0.3× volatility0.05% of price. A percentage, not a volatility multiple — see the note below, because the two behave very differently across names
Nothing about market structureA whole subsystem — break of structure, change of character, a trend state machine and a rolling event counter. Sections 05 and 06
Why the gap threshold change matters

A volatility-scaled minimum asks “is this gap big for this stock”. A percentage minimum asks “is this gap big” full stop. On a quiet large-cap, 0.05% is a fraction of a normal day and almost every gap qualifies; on a volatile small-cap it is noise-level too. The filter is far more permissive than the old one on calm names, which is why the display caps the count rather than relying on the threshold to do the pruning.

02Order blocks

The last opposite-direction candle before a decisive move. The premise is that institutions filled there, and unfilled orders remain when price returns.

TypeFormationRole
Bullish blockThe last down candle before an up impulseSupport on a pullback
Bearish blockThe last up candle before a down impulseResistance — and a natural exit target for a long

The box is drawn body only, open to close. The wick is excluded deliberately: the body is where business was transacted, the wick is where price visited and left.

Mitigation is the whole discipline here

When price closes through a block, its orders are considered filled and the zone is spent. By default the box is removed from the chart entirely rather than greyed.

That deletion is not cosmetic. A mitigated block has no predictive value, and leaving it visible invites you to trade a level that has already done its work. The setting to keep them exists for study, not for trading.

Only five blocks are shown at once, newest first. That cap is the real filter on chart clutter — not the detection threshold.

03Fair value gaps

A three-bar inefficiency: price moved so hard that the middle bar's range never overlapped its neighbours, leaving a band nobody traded.

TypeStructureRole
Bullish gapThe current low sits above the high from two bars backSupport; price tends to return and fill it
Bearish gapThe current high sits below the low from two bars backResistance

The box anchors to the middle bar and extends right until filled. Filled gaps are auto-removed, ten shown at most.

A gap is the precision layer, not the location layer — it narrows a wide block into a few points of entry. Read on its own it is far too common to be a signal, which is exactly what the permissive threshold in section 01 implies.

04Liquidity sweeps

Price pierces a prior swing extreme — taking out the stops clustered there — then rejects and closes back inside. It is the same event Wyckoff calls a spring, described in different language.

SweepConditionRead
Below a swing lowThe low breaks it, the close does notStops taken, sellers exhausted — the long trigger
Above a swing highThe high breaks it, the close does notSupply above — an exit signal in a long-only book
The one combination worth waiting for

A sweep whose wick lands inside a bullish order block, closing back above the swept level. Location and trigger in one bar, with the stop obvious just below the block. Everything else in this module is context; this is the setup.

The same event reaches the bull screener independently as its own quality term, so a sweep is not only a chart annotation — it already contributes to what gets shortlisted.

05Break of structure versus change of character

The subsystem the old guide omits entirely, and the one that actually feeds the score. It keeps a running trend flag and watches price cross the most recent confirmed swing.

EventFires whenMeaning
Break of structure, upClose crosses above the last swing high while already in an uptrendContinuation — the trend did what it was supposed to
Change of character, upThe same cross while in a downtrendReversal. The first sign the other side has taken control
Break of structure, downClose crosses below the last swing low while in a downtrendContinuation lower
Change of character, downThe same cross while in an uptrendThe first warning an advance has failed

Same price event, opposite meanings — what separates them is the trend state at the time, not the bar. That is why the flag has to be carried between bars, and why the port could not be vectorised.

The load-bearing quirk in the port

The change-of-character test reads the trend flag before that same bar updates it — so it is evaluated against the prior bar's trend. Reorder those two lines and every reversal silently becomes a continuation.

The Python port reproduces this deliberately, along with two others from the Wyckoff side, and pins all three with regression tests verified to go red under mutation. It is not a bug to be cleaned up — it is the definition.

06Structure health — and the tier that cannot fire

Changes of character are counted over a rolling twenty bars and graded:

CountLabelReality
0–1CLEANWhat roughly 90% of names read, always
2–3CHOPPYIn practice means exactly 2 — 3 has never been observed
4+BROKENUnreachable. It has never rendered.
Measured, not assumed

Across 38 board names, on daily and 75-minute, at swing lengths 5, 7 and 10 — the count never reached 3 in any cell. Two or more occurred on 3% of daily names and 11–13% of intraday ones.

Two consequences follow. The absence of BROKEN is not evidence that no name is broken — the label simply cannot appear. And a gate keyed on a count of three or more, which an earlier version proposed, would never have fired even once. That is why the structure-health downgrade was dropped rather than shipped.

The row stays as display. Re-tiering the thresholds to make the labels look better would break parity between the two surfaces for a cosmetic gain — and there is still no evidence that a higher count predicts a worse trade, which is what promoting it would require. Change both surfaces together or neither.

07What it contributes

TermValue
Trend direction±2
A fresh change of character, in the same direction±1

Small, and deliberately so. The bonus rewards a reversal that has just happened rather than the standing trend — a change of character twenty bars ago is history, and the freshness test is what stops it being scored as if it were news.

Before the Python port existed, the board did not compute any of this. Structure direction was the path name — bull or recovery — so every recovery candidate was penalised four points for being a recovery candidate, and the change-of-character count was read from a field nothing ever produced, so structure health was permanently CLEAN feeding a constant into the total. The port replaced three proxies with the real thing.

The FVG is the one output that reaches the entry gate — at pattern grade

Everything else on this page grades. The fair value gap gates: a daily or weekly FVG satisfies the LOCATION pillar on its own, ranked alongside a leg-base-leg demand zone rather than below it.

EvidenceGradeWhat it takes to satisfy location
Leg-base-leg zonePatternStands alone
Fair value gap (D/W)PatternStands alone — this module's one gating output
Pivot shelf or pivot linePivotNeeds a confirming S/R level or anchored VWAP
Order block · sweep · BOS · CHoCH—Never gate. They grade, and the change-of-character bonus is deliberately small
Why an unfilled gap earns that rank

Both a demand zone and an unfilled gap mark an imbalance that has not been resolved, and both carry an edge that defines where you are wrong. A pivot shelf marks only that price turned once — which is why it is the one form of location that has to be corroborated.

The practical consequence: turning the levels display off does not merely hide the gaps, it removes a way for a name to pass location. That is the opposite of how a display toggle usually behaves, and it is worth knowing before you switch it.

08The workflow

  1. Take the unmitigated bullish blocks below price. Those are the pullback targets. Ones above price are irrelevant as support.
  2. Rank them. A block sitting on a high-volume node or a major average is worth more than one in open air; a recent block is worth more than a hundred-bar-old one; and the bigger the move that left it, the more was transacted there.
  3. Narrow with a gap. A bullish gap inside the block is the precise entry — stop below the block's low, not inside it.
  4. Prefer a sweep as the trigger. Section 04. Enter on the close of the sweep bar or the first bar after the rejection.
  5. Use bearish blocks above as targets. If an engine target lands on one, that is where to take the partial.

Nothing here generates a trade on its own. The blocks and gaps feed the location gate; the sweep feeds a quality term. The decision belongs to the screeners and the entry gate.

09Where it sits in the funnel

This layer is micro-precision inside a macro frame, and it is worth being explicit about the order, because using it alone is the classic failure:

MACRO — is this even tradeable
    stage 2 · above the 30-week average · relative strength positive

TIMING — is it ready
    the average stack aligned · a contraction or a pullback

PRECISION — where exactly, and when
    at an unmitigated bullish block
    a gap inside it for the price
    or a sweep at or below it for the trigger

A bullish order block in a stage-4 decline is a trap, and it looks identical to one in a stage-2 advance. The layer has no opinion about which it is drawing — that is what the layers above it are for.

The correspondences are worth holding: a sweep is a Wyckoff spring; a block retest is a secondary test; the recovery book's climax turn-bar is structurally the same event as a bullish sweep confirmation. Three vocabularies, one behaviour.

One combination to avoid

Keep the block swing length at or below the structure swing length. Above it, blocks are anchored to larger pivots than the ones defining structure, and the two subsystems end up describing different charts.

Tighter is available — a longer block swing and a bigger gap minimum give fewer, higher-quality zones. Looser is available too, and mostly produces more boxes rather than more information.

11Mistakes

MistakeWhy it costs you
Trading every blockOnly ones aligned with the stage. A bullish block in a decline is a trap that looks exactly like the real thing
Trading a mitigated zonePrice already closed through it; the orders are filled. The default removes it for this reason
Putting the stop inside the blockInside is where absorption happens. You get taken out by the very activity that makes the zone work — the stop belongs below its low
Chasing a sweepEnter on the sweep bar's close or the first bar after. Five bars later the move has happened and your stop is far away
Reading the absence of BROKEN as healthSection 06. That tier has never rendered and cannot
Confusing a break with a change of characterSame bar, opposite meanings. One says the trend continued, the other says it turned
