Positioning Basis (Trapped Money Calculator)
  • Long Basis (basis L): Weighted price of contracts added on up-close days.
    • Price < basis L: Marginal long is underwater; sells to break even (Resistance).
    • Price > basis L: Longs are in profit; actively buy to defend dips (Support).
  • Short Basis (basis S): Weighted price where contracts were added on down-closes. Price sustaining above it traps shorts, generating covering acceleration fuel.
  • Built Share (bld %): Share of 20 daily prints adding net contracts.
    • ≥ 40%: Active, mathematically reliable book.
    • < 40%: Book mostly unwound; basis is stale (suppress read).

4. Derivatives Traps & False-Signal Shields Failure Mode Protection
TRAP 1: The Short Covering Mirage (Price ▲, OI ▼)
A green breakout bar looks identical to genuine institutional accumulation, but falling OI reveals it is powered purely by trapped bears buying to cover. When covering exhausts, bids vanish and price rolls over. Shield: Never buy breakout highs on short covering; buy only pullbacks to support.
TRAP 2: The Call Wall Suffocation (Call Wall < 1.5R away)
A perfect Stage 2 chart setup forms with a 3.0R target, but peak call writing sits just 3% above entry. Call writers defend their strike aggressively by shorting futures, stalling the trade before T1. Shield: Always verify "R to the wall". If under 2R, demand a deeper discount entry.
TRAP 3: The Trapped-Long Selling Cascade (Price < Long Basis)
When price breaks down below basis L, every marginal buyer in the futures book is in the red. The first test of basis L is routinely swamped by breakeven selling. Shield: Treat basis L as hard resistance until price closes above it on expanding volume.
TRAP 4: Expiry Week Gamma Pinning (Last 5 Days to Expiry)
Expecting a 5R trending runner during monthly expiry week when spot is within 1.5% of Max Pain. Market maker delta hedging forces price to oscillate around Max Pain to burn premium. Shield: Trade swing size; close or scale out before Thursday expiry.
5. House Target Matrix & R-Canon Scaling Disciplined Scale-Out Rules
HOUSE SCALE-OUT ALLOCATION (3-LEG RULE):
• Leg 1 (25% at T1 / Call Wall): Covers risk; moves trade to free-roll.
• Leg 2 (25% at T2): Locks solid equity profit.
• Leg 3 (50% Runner on Trail): 88% of positional trade gains come from the trailing runner. T3 defaults to 0 to protect the runner!

R to Wall = (Call Wall - Entry Price) / (Entry Price - Stop Loss)
