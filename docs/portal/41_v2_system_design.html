The v2 System

The v2 system: a weekly ranked portfolio (Clenow, ensemble rank) run beside the current system. Jay's answers became the house book V2H; the costly rules were dropped, ETFs far from NAV are kept out, and four v1 rules that passed their test were borrowed in.

1. What v2 is, in one paragraph

A weekly ranked portfolio of NSE stocks, based on Andreas Clenow's Stocks on the Move. Once a week it ranks every eligible stock by the quality of its trend, holds the strongest, sizes each position so all carry the same risk, sells what drops out of the top of the ranking, and stops buying altogether when the market itself is in a downtrend. It does not try to time the perfect entry — five tests showed that timing adds nothing here — it decides what to own and how much.

5. Data flow and schedule

  1. Nightly (auto-pilot Phase 12i): p1_paper.py loads two years of daily bars for the Nifty 500, fills last week's orders at the open (Monday), marks every book at each close, writes the rank board data/v2/board.csv.
  2. On a week's last close: decides the next orders for every book, writes the order list, sends the Telegram summary, and posts P1E's buys to the receiver as SYMBOL S4 GO D - v2.
  3. The reviewer reads the S4/S5 panels for each name on the Daily chart and gets a V2 CONTEXT block: the selection is done, judge execution only (extension, earnings in the next 5 sessions, a wall within 1 ATR, gaps, illiquidity). Rulings land in the Reviewer Log with source v2.
  4. Monday's open: P1E buys everything; P1ER skips the names ruled WAIT/PASS.
  5. The v2 page shows the board, books, orders + reviews, positions and the trade blotter.

Code: p1_paper.py (engine), pro_systems_backtest.p1_weekly (the rules — the same function the backtests ran, so paper and backtest cannot drift), v2_core.py (board, reviewer context, review lookups), commander_pages/v2_system.py (the page).

6. What we expect (honest numbers)

Survivorship-corrected backtest, 2018–2026, costs included: CAGR ~19 %, worst drawdown ~22 %, return ÷ drawdown ~0.86 (P1); the robustness grid says to plan on ~0.75. The ensemble P1E read 0.94 on the same data but is judged forward only. Benchmarks: Nifty 500 0.29, Momentum-30 replica 0.40. Expect long spells in cash when the market is below its 200-day SMA — that is the rule working, not failing.

7. How v2 gets fine-tuned (the rule for changes)

  • Every change is a pre-registered forward test: a new paper book with the change, run beside P1E, judged on data after it starts. No tuning on data already seen.
  • First reads: 16 Jan 2027 (three months) and 16 Apr 2027 (six months, scheduled).
  • Candidate changes already on the list: the cut-off (20 % vs 25 % vs 30 %), a sector cap, a catastrophic stop (see Q5), Wednesday vs Friday rebalancing, and an earnings-momentum factor (point-in-time data now exists from 2024).

8. Open design questions — answered by Jay on 18 Oct (see §9 for what the test said)

  1. Universe. Live v2 ranks today's Nifty 500. The survivorship test used the 500 most-traded NSE names point-in-time. Keep the Nifty 500, or rank the top 500 by turnover (adds names before they enter the index)?
  2. Capital split. When both systems trade real money, how much goes to each? (Proposal: v2 starts at one-third of trading capital per Doc 40; the current system keeps the rest.)
  3. Rebalance day. Friday close → Monday open (today), or Wednesday as Clenow uses (avoids the Monday gap)?
  4. Market filter. 200-day SMA only (tested), or also the house exposure tiers (untested for v2)?
  5. Stops. v2 has no hard stop — exits are weekly. The DNA says ATR stops are mandatory. Options: (a) keep it as tested; (b) add a wide catastrophic stop (e.g. entry − 4 × ATR or −15 %) on the live book only; (c) test (b) as a forward paper book first. (Recommendation: c.)
  6. The reviewer's veto. Advisory only (P1E ignores it, P1ER obeys it) until P1E vs P1ER shows whether it helps — agreed?
  7. Position count. At 0.10 % risk per ATR the book holds ~25–40 names. Comfortable, or would you rather fewer, larger positions (higher risk per name)?
  8. Taxes. The backtest ignores tax. Weekly rotation means most gains are short-term (20 % STCG). Should the live version prefer holding past one year where the rank is close (a tax-aware tie-break)?
  9. ETFs. v2 ranks stocks only. Should sector/index ETFs compete in the same rank?

How stocks and ETFs are ranked (Q9)

  • Never in one rank. An ETF is a basket, so its trend is smoother (higher R²) and its ATR lower: in a joint rank ETFs would crowd the top in calm markets and equal-risk sizing would make them 2–3× larger than a stock. An Indian index ETF also duplicates the stocks already ranked.
  • Two pools, one score. The same ensemble trend-quality score, as a percentile within its own pool. ETF pool: the curated tradeable list (etf_universe, no debt), one per underlying (the most traded), median turnover ≥ ₹2 crore; above the 100-DMA; no 15 % single-day move in 90 sessions.
  • Hold the top 25 % of eligible ETFs (at least 2, at most 5), ≤ 10 % of equity each, ≤ 30 % in all, bought after the stocks from the cash they leave.
  • Regime per asset class. Indian-equity ETFs follow the stock rules; gold, silver and international ETFs follow only their own trend (close above their own 200-day SMA). This is what lets the book hold something useful while Indian equities are in a downtrend.
  • Today (16 Oct): 4 ETFs eligible; only MAFANG (NYSE FANG+) qualifies as a non-Indian ETF; gold and silver are below both their 100- and 200-day averages, so they are correctly out.
  • NAV premium (Jay, 18 Oct): an ETF trading more than 3 % away from its NAV is out of the pool. International ETFs currently sit at +14 % to +105 % over NAV (creations suspended at the overseas limit), so their price measures the premium, not the holdings. On 16 Oct this removes MAFANG (+53.8 %) and ITBEES (+3.7 %, likely T-1 NAV noise); gold and silver are within ±1.2 %.

v1 rules borrowed into v2 (18 Oct, PREREG_v2_borrowed_rules)

  • Borrowed as is (protective, live): NAV-premium gate, ETF liquidity floor, earnings deferral (a buy waits a week when results are due within 7 days), stale-data keep (a missing bar never triggers a sell), NSE holiday calendar, the reviewer as an execution check.
  • Tested and adopted: sector cap 25 %, correlation block 0.90, the ₹1 lakh per-trade cap (3.33 % of equity), the house Chandelier trail on top of the 4 × ATR disaster stop. Together:

17.3 % CAGR, max DD −20.1 %, MAR 0.74 · 1.12 (V2H before them 16.8 %, −20.8 %, 0.71 · 1.05).

  • Tested and rejected: weekly Stage 1/2 only (churn, deeper drawdown), the 6 % open-risk budget and the 25-position limit (both starve a 30–40 name book). Decision open for Jay: whether those two house limits apply to real v2 money.
