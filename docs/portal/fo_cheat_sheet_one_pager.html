Positioning Basis (Trapped Money Calculator)
  • Long Basis (basis L): Weighted price of contracts bought on up-closes.
    • If Price < basis L: Marginal long is underwater; sells to break even (Resistance).
    • If Price > basis L: Longs are in profit; buy to defend dips (Support).
  • Short Basis (basis S): Weighted price of contracts sold on down-closes. Price sustaining above it traps shorts, creating short-covering acceleration fuel.
  • Built Share (bld %): Share of 20 daily prints adding net contracts. ≥40% = active reliable book; <40% = stale, unwinding book (ignore).

Institutional Ceiling
  • Put-Call Ratio (PCR): Total Put OI ÷ Total Call OI.
    • < 0.70 Call-Heavy 🔴: Massive call writing. Rallies capped.
    • 0.70 – 1.30 Balanced ⚪: Neutral tape; no strong derivatives bias.
    • > 1.30 Put-Heavy 🟢: Writers underwriting downside; strong floor.
  • Call Wall (writers R): Strike with peak Call OI. A concrete ceiling chart patterns cannot see. If T1 sits above it, your reward is fighting option writers.
  • Put Wall (writers S): Strike with peak Put OI. Validates stop-loss placement if your SL rests safely behind this wall.
  • Max Pain: Strike where option buyers lose maximum money.
    • Rule: An expiry magnet, NEVER a price target. Pulls price inward during expiry week.
  • ATM Shift (ATM ΔOI): + = Writers adding (consolidation drift); − = Writers fleeing (path clear for drive).
The 4-Step Execution Protocol (Band V Plan & Risk)
STEP 1: STATE CHECK — Is it Long Build-up?
• YES: Enter with full aggression. • NO (Short Build-up / Covering): Enter only on pullbacks to value.
STEP 2: BASIS HURDLE — Is price below basis L?
• YES: Expect immediate selling churn at basis L (trapped longs scratching breakeven).
• NO: basis L acts as active support beneath; anchor your trailing plan to it.
STEP 3: CALL WALL VS T1 — Does writers R sit below technical T1?
• YES: Check R to the wall. Plan mandatory partial scale-out (25%–50%) at or before the wall!
• NO: Clear air to T1; hold standard runner for 3R+.
STEP 4: STOP SHIELDING — Does your Stop Loss rest below the Put Wall (writers S)?
• YES: Institutionally protected: put writers must be overrun before your stop is threatened.
• NO: Unshielded stop: widen stop past the put wall or trim size.
