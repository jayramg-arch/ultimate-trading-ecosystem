The numbering has one collision. Both the Golden Rules and the Scanner Filter Map are filed as 25 in docs/; the Scanner Filter Map is marked 25* here. Renumbering would break the filename links, so it is flagged rather than fixed.

Local files only, since 20 Sep. The claude.ai copies are retired — a republish cost a full re-read of the page, and the local file is always the current one.

Pages are revised against the code, never against the notes — seven on 11 September, five more on 16 September, and 09/22/23/25/26 again on 20 September after the second audit. The table at the top says what moved. If a page and the chart still disagree, the chart is right and the page is due.
