01Three corrections, verified against the code

This guide predates several changes to the engine it documents. All three were checked at source while this page was written.

1 · The base test is SIX gates, not nine

The guide describes a nine-gate checklist and says all nine must pass. The code requires six: stage · trend alignment · relative strength · sector stage · volume accumulation · trend template.

Three gates were deliberately removed — the Stage-2 freshness check (demoted to something you eyeball rather than a blocker), and both squeeze gates. The squeeze removal was the big one: a squeeze is mutually exclusive with a breakout, so requiring both nullified the entire positional book. Any nine-gate reference below, including the CSV proxy table, is historical.

2 · The alpha score is price action, not RSI and ADX

The score table still lists RSI(14) > 60 and ADX > 20. Those were converted: momentum is now close against its own 10-bar and 5-bar values, and direction is a count of up-closes making higher highs. The point values are unchanged — only the mechanism moved, so the thresholds you know still hold.

3 · The per-catalyst alpha figures predate the horizon fix

The numbers quoted here were measured before a benchmarking error was found — trades were being compared against their full design window while most had exited far earlier. Correcting it cut headline alpha by roughly two thirds. Treat the per-catalyst ordering as indicative and the magnitudes as too high; the current honest position is at the bottom of this page.

02The price-action conversion

The single biggest change in the engine's history. Per the standing rule — replace lagging indicators with pure price action wherever possible — every catalyst gate and the alpha score were moved off oscillators onto price-action equivalents, and all chart surfaces were synced up to Python.

The structural bugs this exposed

BugEffect, and the fix
Squeeze gate in the base testA coil and a breakout are mutually exclusive, so requiring both produced zero positional picks for twenty-four months. Removed
Pullback quality strippedIt was firing on weak, rolling-over stocks. The pattern worth carrying

Three of those four were silent — they produced no error, just an empty or degraded result set. A catalyst that never fires looks exactly like a market with no setups. The funnel diagnostic that counts survivors at each stage is what found them.

Confluence is not a green light. Several signals agreeing can just as easily mean several signals confirming a bad macro setup. Check stage and relative strength first.

Which to use

The gate score always governs whether a catalyst fires. The composite is what you rank by — it captures quality the way the backtest does. They are a filter and a sort, not two opinions.

05The base gate — six conditions

Every positional catalyst sits on this test. All six must hold.

stage_ok
Weekly Stage 2, or Stage 1 for an early entry. Only trade in the right macro stage
trend_aligned
Above the long-term average — the trend-template baseline
rs_ok
Sector relative strength not lagging. Do not buy a leader in a sinking sector
sector_stage_ok
The sector itself is in Stage 1 or 2
vol_acc_ok
Enough recent bars closing green in the upper part of range on above-average volume — the accumulation footprint
trend_template_ok
Within range of the 52-week high and well above the 52-week low — the growth-stock prerequisite

What used to be here, and why it went

Removed gateWhy
stage2_fresh_okDemoted from a blocker to a status you eyeball. Maturity is a sizing question, not an eligibility one
ma_sqz_okA squeeze is mutually exclusive with a breakout. Requiring compression and expansion on the same bar produced zero positional picks for two years
bb_sqz_ok

A name showing five of six is a high-priority watch even when the catalyst has not fired — that is the intended way to read a near-miss.

06Catalyst logic

Folded per catalyst. Each sits on top of the base gate and the alpha floor.

POS-AC · accumulation before the move
on-balance volume breaking to a 20-bar high
AND institutional volume on an accumulation bar
AND price has NOT run yet — still meaningfully below the 63-bar high
AND the alpha gate passes

Finds the volume footprint before the price breakout. The price-lag condition is what makes it accumulation rather than a late chase — and the anti-chase test now caps how far above its own recent close the bar may sit.

POS-BO · the Stage-2 breakout
the full base gate
AND close above the 21-day high
AND close in the top quarter of today's range
AND volume above average
AND the alpha gate passes

The core positional edge. Note the range condition — a breakout that closes weak is not a breakout, it is a rejection at the level.

SWG-PB · the pullback
trend template active — the full moving-average stack
AND price close to the short average
AND retrace 38-62% of the 20-bar range   (was an RSI pocket)
AND volume dry-up
AND close above the prior day's high — entry confirmation
AND the micro edge is active
AND the alpha gate passes

The confirmation clause is the important one. Without it the catalyst fired into falling dips on weak, rolling-over names. You are entering on resumption, not on the way down. The stop is a structural floor at the short average rather than a fixed multiple that got whipsawed out.

Regime-sensitive. This is a momentum-continuation setup and it underperforms in corrective tapes — it is the right tool only in a confirmed uptrend.

SWG-BO · the contraction breakout
volatility compression present
AND close above the 15-bar pivot high
AND anti-algo: bar range under twice ATR
AND volume well above average
AND the alpha gate passes

The anti-algo filter is the distinctive part: it rejects abnormally wide bars, which are more often stop-hunts than genuine breakouts. If it fails while price has broken out, wait for the next bar to confirm rather than entering into the spike.

SWG-REV · mean reversion
prior down-structure plus a bullish reversal bar today   (was RSI below 20)
AND stage 1 or 2 — not a downtrend
AND no genuine weekly weakness
AND above the medium-term average
AND the alpha gate passes

Catches short-term pullbacks inside healthy uptrends. The rewrite fixed a contradiction that made the original condition impossible to satisfy: it demanded a down close and a close above the prior high on the same bar.

GAP-GO · the gap that holds
gap of 4% or more
AND volume at least three times average — an institutional gap
AND close in the upper 60% of the day's range
AND the alpha gate passes

Do not trade this at the open. The catalyst requires the stock to hold its gains — entering before that is confirmed is speculation, not system trading.

07Catalyst-aware stops

The stop distance scales with the trade's design horizon. Risk per trade is unchanged — so a wider stop means a smaller position, which is the correct discipline rather than a bigger bet.

FamilyATR multipleTypical distanceWhy
POS-BO · POS-ACCUM4.0×~6–12%A multi-month hold needs room for ordinary volatility
Wyckoff base3.5×~5–10%Multi-month base structure
Recovery2.5×~4–7%A quarter-length horizon
Swing family1.5×~2–4%Short horizon — unchanged
Why this mattered

Every catalyst previously used the swing multiple. A positional trade carrying a swing-sized stop gets knocked out by routine pullbacks inside the first fortnight — the signal was fine and the exit threw the edge away.

Verify the stop before you order

The ATR rule is a fallback for when no structural anchor exists. In practice the natural stop sits just below the breakout pivot, or below the accumulation base low. If your structural stop is tighter, use it — and reduce size to keep risk constant. Only if it would sit above the current close do you fall back to the multiple.

08The liquidity-sweep bonus

A separate quality bonus rather than a catalyst of its own. When price wicks below the 5-day low and then closes back above it — and above the medium-term average, on expanded volume — the alpha score receives a +20 bonus, capped at the ceiling.

swept below the recent low  AND  closed back above the 50-period average
AND volume well above average     →   alpha += 20

This is the market-structure read folded directly into quality scoring: stops below a visible low were hunted and the move reversed. Combined with an accumulation footprint it is one of the more reliable entry patterns in the system — and because it lands in the score rather than the catalyst, it lifts a marginal name over the gate rather than firing a signal on its own.

The rule this table encodes

One owner per concept, everyone else mirrors verbatim. When a threshold changes here, it must reach every consumer in the same change — zero signal drift is the DNA-level rule, and the reason a chart reading and a pipeline reading can be trusted to agree.

Pitfalls when reading the file
  • It is a snapshot at scan time, not real-time. Price may have moved a percent or two before you open it — always re-quote before ordering.
  • No catalyst does not mean skip. It means no signal today. With high alpha and a good stage, those are tomorrow's candidates.
  • A flagged catalyst with no confirming signal means the setup exists but the close has not happened. Do not front-run it.
  • Relative strength is on a scaled measure, not a raw ratio — a value of fifteen means fifteen points of outperformance, not fifteen times.
  • Two RS columns showing the same number is not a bug — it happens when the sector index is the broad index.
One thing the CSV cannot give you

The gate breakdown is not exported as individual columns. Use confluence as the proxy and open the chart when a name matters — and disregard any nine-gate proxy mapping, which predates the reduction to six.

14Daily workflow

WhenWhat you do
Pre-marketRun the scan, sort by score, and cut anything failing the alpha floor, a Stage 3/4, or a lagging sector — before you look at a single chart
After the openWatch the confirming flags on names already in the right regime. A flagged catalyst is not an entry until its trigger flips
Post-marketSave the file with a date stamp. Over time that builds a hit-rate database — which catalysts, personas and quadrants actually deliver

15Common mistakes

  1. Acting on a catalyst below the alpha floor. The gate is mandatory — a breakout scoring 45 has several quality factors missing, so the setup is incomplete rather than early.
  2. Ignoring the persona. An avoid classification means the macro backdrop is wrong, whatever the pattern looks like.
  3. Trading the gap catalyst at the open. It requires the stock to hold its gains. Entering before that is speculation.
  4. Treating confluence as an automatic entry. Multiple signals can all be confirming the same bad macro setup.
  5. Skipping the anti-algo check. Contraction breakouts on wide bars frequently fail — wait for the next bar rather than entering the spike.
  6. Reading a nine-gate panel as current. The base test is six; three gates were removed deliberately.
  7. Using a swing-sized stop on a positional catalyst. That is what threw the edge away before stops became horizon-aware.

The honest current position

After the horizon correction, the system-wide figure is roughly +0.5% to +1.0% matched alpha per trade, with the probability of positive true alpha near 50% and a confidence interval that straddles zero. The median trade loses to the index — this is a low-hit-rate, big-winner profile.

Older reports quoting much larger cumulative figures, or a badly negative Sharpe, were both measurement artifacts and should be disregarded. Plan around the smaller number.
