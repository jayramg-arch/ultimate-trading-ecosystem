This is the tick-box distillation of the Trading Bible's 9-step confluence sequence, the bull and recovery exit tables, and position sizing — mapped to the modules you actually load and run.

Three nuances to internalise — read once, they are not bugs

1 · The Pine catalyst trigger is confirmation, not a gate. The *_trigger booleans encode the backtest gates, so they stay silent most of the time even on a clean, tradeable setup. A silent trigger is not a veto. If Steps 1–7 pass and you can see the edge's documented entry conditions with your own eyes, you take it discretionarily (Step 8, Path B). What discretion never overrides: the hard floors of Steps 1–7 and the sizing and stop discipline of Step 9. Judgement applies to entry timing — never to regime, conviction, or risk.

2 · POS-ACCUM is enabled everywhere, and is now pure price action. The anti-chase gate is no longer an RSI cap: dailyRsi ≤ 50 was replaced by close ≤ close[5] × 1.05, and its VCP loosened to f_is_vcp(1.5) so quiet accumulation is not starved. In the 24-month validation it is the strongest catalyst. Any older note describing an RSI gate — or the “disabled” comment in the .pine header — is stale; trust the code.

3 · Exit-table doc lag. The guides say v3.6 widened the trails (POS-BO Chandelier 3.0×→4.5×, swing trail EMA20→SMA50, targets 5R/10R→3R/5R at 33%), but their body tables still show the legacy values — as does the exit section here. Trust the input defaults in your loaded script. Exits are automated, not hand-entered.

How to use it

Two tracks, one funnel

Swing (8–12 weeks, 5–8%) and positional (6–8 months, 10–30%) run the same nine steps. The track changes only the catalyst, the exit row, and the risk percentage.

Hard floors are hard

A failed HARD FLOOR is no trade, no matter how strong everything downstream looks. Bypassing a floor is bypassing the system.

Order is one-directional

Macro → Sector → Watchlist → Stock. If Step 1 or 2 fails, stop — do not go on to score the stock.

Swing vs positional — the checklist decides, not you

You do not pick the track up front. It resolves in three stages:

StageWhereWhat it does
LeanStep 2 / 3.5 — RRG tierLEADING + RS-Ratio ≥ 100 → positional lean. IMPROVING + RS-Ratio < 100 → swing lean. Bias only, not a commitment.
LockStep 4 — the catalystThe decision point. The catalyst is the horizon: POS-BO / POS-AC = positional (120/180-day window); SWG-PB / SWG-BO / GAP-GO = swing (30-day); REV-* = recovery. Make the firm call here.
OperationaliseSteps 8–9Positional → Cat-2 MTF, room ≥ 20% ROI, POS exit row, risk 0.75%. Swing → Cat-1 MTF, room ≥ 2R, SWG exit row, risk 0.75%.
Tie-breaker

If a positional catalyst and a swing structure both appear, default to the catalyst's horizon — never cut a positional winner short with a swing-sized target.

When the trigger is silent, ask why before going discretionary

If it is just the algo's strict margin (ADX 23 not 25, vol 1.4× not 1.5×) on an otherwise clean setup, Path B is sound — size normally. If a real condition is missing (no breakout, RS LAGGING, macro RED, structure LH-LL) that is a Step 1–7 floor failure, not a trigger quirk, and you do not override it. A quiet trigger is fine; a failed hard floor is not.

Conviction tiering: Path A with 9/9 gates → full size · clean Path B (floors pass, 1–2 algo gates marginal) → normal size · Path B with any soft floor → half size.

8b

Execution toolkit — demand & supply entries

Feeds Step 8 Path B · long-only, no intraday

A second, complementary system: pure price-action demand/supply zones, key levels, and EMA20 as dynamic support. The quant funnel decides which stock; this toolkit decides where and when you enter, where the stop sits, and whether there is room. These are named, selectable Path B triggers — they never relax Steps 1–7 or Step 9.

Shared vocabulary

  • Zone construction — legin → base (1–6 candles, sweet spot 2–4) → legout. RBR/DBR are bullish demand zones. Proximal is the edge nearest price; distal is the far edge and your stop anchor.
  • Controlling / WCDZ — a weekly controlling demand zone that produced the move creating the prevailing structure; the highest grade of demand. Fresh = untested since formation. Strong = strong legout plus a gap or multi-pivot break.
  • MTTWR / MTTWS — multiple-times-tested weak resistance or support: a level tested so often it is primed to break.
  • MTF pairing — Cat-1: HTF monthly/weekly/daily → LTF 125m/75m. Tighter stop, more entries. Cat-2: HTF monthly/weekly → LTF daily. Wider stop, cleaner signals, better for positional.
  • Room rule — only take it if the path to the nearest opposing supply zone gives ≥ 20% ROI (positional) or clears R:R ≥ 2.0R (swing). No room, no trade, however clean the entry.

How it wires into the funnel

Zone-aware stop → Step 9. Use the distal as the structural stop. Fall back to catalyst-aware ATR (POS 4.0× / SWG 1.5×) only if the distal sits above entry.
Room → Gate 3. Measure clear air to the nearest overhead supply. ≥ 20% ROI (positional) or R:R ≥ 2.0R (swing), or skip. This is the Gate-3 check, measured zone to zone.
Zone quality → Step 6. Fresh + strong + controlling is highest confidence; a tested or weak zone is a half-size signal at best.
Long-only, no intraday. Setup 3 is a trim tool, not an entry. Setup 9 is out of scope entirely.
One funnel, two lenses

The quant edges and these D&S patterns point at the same trade from different angles. When a Pine catalyst and a zone agree — pos_ac_trigger plus an engulf in a fresh WCDZ — that is your highest-conviction, full-size entry.
