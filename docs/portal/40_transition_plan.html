1. The current book (legacy positions) — Jay's decision: hold for recovery

Jay is choosing to hold the current positions for a recovery and close some near break-even, because a further drawdown on capital is not affordable. That is his call. The plan only asks that holding is done with the protections that make it survivable:

  • The Disaster SL stays resting at Dhan on every holding and is never cancelled or widened to "give it room". Holding for recovery means not selling at the thesis level; it does not mean removing the floor under the position.
  • No averaging down on a losing holding.
  • A review date, not an open-ended wait: each holding is looked at again at the first weekly close with the Nifty 500 back above its 200-day SMA, and in any case on 15 Jan 2027.
  • A note to keep beside it: the market does not know the entry price. At each review the question is "would I buy this today with this money?" — the break-even level is not part of that answer.

2. New entries — none until the market recovers

"Recovered" is defined mechanically so the decision is not made in the moment:

  • the Nifty 500 closes a week above its 200-day SMA (P1's own regime gate), and
  • the exposure rule's tier is NEUTRAL or OPEN (regime score 3+).

Until both hold, no new entries in either system. The P1 paper book enforces the first by itself.

3. Path to real money for the v2 core

P1 is paper-only today (robustness failed). Real money needs all of these:

  1. Three months of paper (from 16 Oct 2026 → 16 Jan 2027 at the earliest).
  2. The market has recovered (definition above).
  3. The paper book has done its job: since its start, P1 paper is at or above the Nifty 500 and its drawdown is shallower than the index's.
  4. A forward pre-registration that answers the robustness doubt. Proposed: a third paper book, P1E — an ensemble (the average rank of the 60/90/120-session lookbacks, a 25 % cut-off, 0.10 % risk), registered now and judged only on data from now on. Ensembles are the standard professional answer to parameter risk: no single setting has to be right.

Stages once cleared: one-third of the trading capital into the chosen book → two-thirds after three more months if the live book tracks its paper twin (within 3 percentage points) → full. Each stage is reversible: a live drawdown deeper than the backtest's worst (−19 % for P1, −24 % allowed for the ensemble) stops the scale-up.
