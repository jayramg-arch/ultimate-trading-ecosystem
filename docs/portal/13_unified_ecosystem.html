01One contradiction, flagged not resolved

The header canon and the body tables disagree about targets and trails

The v3.5 rulings at the top of the source guide set the R-canon — positional 3R/5R, swing 2R/4R — and confirm the trail multiple at 4.5. The detailed exit tables further down still show the legacy ladder (2.5R and 3.5R) and a 3.0 trail.

The header is the later and evidenced position. This page states the canon in the exit section and marks the legacy figures as superseded — but if you are reading the source markdown, expect to meet both.

And a consequence worth acting on

Strategy Tester results from before the v3.5 change are not comparable. Both the stage (which gates entries) and the target ladder (which sizes exits) moved. Re-run before reading anything into a backtest.

02The v3.5 rulings

Seven weeks of decisions that had landed everywhere else and not here. That gap mattered more in this script than in any other, because this one takes trades.

1 · Stage became a stateless 2×2

The old code ran a hysteresis state machine that evolved the stage from its previous value, plus two overrides letting a separate pivot engine rewrite the answer. The consequence: a name trading above a declining 30-week average never left “Stage 1”. When the same defect was fixed on the dashboard it measured at 19 of 56 names mis-staged — one sat at Stage 1 with its anchor falling 62.9 points, which is distribution, not a base.

above the 30-week average  →  rising ? 2 : falling ? 3 : (RS up ? 1 : 3)
below the 30-week average  →  (falling and RS not up) ? 4 : 1

A flat anchor resolves to 1, however strong relative strength is — a flat anchor is a base, not an advance. Both overrides are gone; the pivot engine survives only as the flat-cell tiebreak.

One refinement deliberately NOT ported

The dashboard labels below a rising anchor as a pullback variant of Stage 2. Here the stage is an integer that feeds the catalyst gates, not a display string — so promoting that cell would silently admit pullback-below-the-anchor names into every Stage-2 gate. Python does not do it either. If you want it, make it a deliberate one-line change, not a copy.

The trade-off, stated rather than hidden

A stateless read can flip between 2 and 3 while the average hovers near flat. The slope threshold is the knob for that — widen the flat band rather than freeze a stale digit.

Together the flat band here was six times the one every other surface used — so an anchor that everything else called rising read flat in the strategy, and blocked entries accordingly.

3 · Rotation recalibrated

Moved onto the shared calibration — decoupled lookbacks with an origin-preserving transform — the same port already in the dashboard, the trigger indicator and the allocator, verified identical to the Python engine to twelve decimal places. The single-lookback input is now ignored by design, because one lookback cannot serve both axes.

4 · The R-canon

Set by a single rule: no trade below 2R, and something always left to trail.

Gap and reversion2R / 2–4R2R / 4R50 / 50

Why the old ladder failed in practice

At 5R, the first target was reached in a tiny minority of positional trades — so the partial, the breakeven move and the trail effectively never engaged. A target you almost never hit is not a target; it is a decoration.

5 · The trail multiple stayed — for a different reason

The old justification was the 5R/10R ladder, and that ladder is gone. It stays on its own evidence: it is the shared brain's positional multiple — so the strategy trails the way your live book does — and four separate stop studies have rejected tightening it.

04Entry criteria, edge by edge

Folded per edge. Each carries its own stop type, trail and time stop — this is where the ten edges actually differ, and reading the wrong one is how a positional stop ends up on a swing trade.

Bull — positional

POS-BO · positional breakout

The cleanest institutional signal — a stock escaping a base into open air.

bull book ON · edge ON
AND base confirmed        (Stage 2 + market bull + the MA stack)
AND alpha above floor
AND rotation leading or improving
AND micro edge            (above the daily pivot and the monthly anchor)
AND close above the 20-bar prior high
AND volume above 1.5x average
AND volume shelf          (the short volume-weighted average above the long)
Stop10-bar structural low with a small ATR pad
TrailChandelier ratchet — upward only
SizeKelly-adjusted, capped by max allocation

The volume-shelf gate is the one people skip: it is what rules out a false volume spike on an otherwise clean-looking breakout.

POS-AC · positional accumulation

Buy during the base itself — before the breakout — when volume flow shows quiet accumulation. Lower risk than the breakout, but it demands a tightening structure.

everything POS-BO requires, minus the breakout, plus:
  accumulation structure   close above the short averages, stacked
  accumulation action      an up bar closing in the top third of its range
  volume                   moderately above average
  loose contraction        range narrowing AND volume below its average
  flow rising              two consecutive rises AND above its own average
  volume shelf
StopSame as the breakout — structural low with a pad
TrailChandelier ratchet, breakeven locked after the first target

Note the deliberate tension: volume must be below average for the contraction test and rising in flow terms. That is the signature of accumulation — quiet price, persistent buying.

Bull — swing

SWG-PB · swing pullback

A continuation entry: price pulls back into the short average, tightens, and closes back above it on a bullish bar.

bull book ON · edge ON · market healthy
AND above the medium average
AND the low touched or pierced the short average
AND close back above it       the recovery, not the dip
AND a bullish close
AND tight contraction         range well under its average
AND the MA stack aligned      the structural filter
AND momentum away from both extremes
AND volume dry-up             prior bars consolidating, not distributing
StopFive-bar swing low less padding
TrailShort-average ratchet, breakeven after the first target
Time stopTen days below half an R
Hard exitA close below the medium average closes it immediately
SWG-BO · swing contraction breakout

A very tight base breaking out on volume — a shorter hold, without the positional prerequisite stack.

bull book ON · edge ON · market healthy
AND tight contraction   range under its average AND volume under its average
AND close above the 20-bar prior high
AND volume above 1.5x average

This edge does not require base confirmation or an alpha floor. It is a pure momentum-and-pattern trade, which is exactly why its hold is short and its stop is a swing stop. Do not size it like a positional entry.

SWG-REV · swing reversion

Mean reversion while the long-term trend is intact.

above the long average          the trend must still be there
AND below the short average     short-term depressed
AND momentum oversold
AND a bullish bar
AND close above the prior bar's high   the reversal confirmation
First targetFixed at 2R — no dynamic adjustment, because a reversion is not a trend trade
Time stopFive days maximum. If it has not reverted, the premise has failed
Hard exitA close below the medium average
GAP-GO · the gap that holds
bull book ON · edge ON · market healthy
AND the open is above the prior high AND the close is above the open
AND the gap is at least 4%
AND the close sits in the top 40% of the gap bar's range
AND volume at least 3x average
StopThe gap bar's low, less padding
TrailShort-average ratchet
Time stopTen days

The intraday-position test is the whole edge: it separates conviction buying from a gap that faded. Everything else is size and volume.

Recovery — legacy

REV-CB · climax bottom

A high-risk, high-reward counter-trend entry. The sequence is: stretched → panic → climax → turn.

PillarCondition
StretchedMaterially down from the 60-bar high
OversoldA washout of red bars, closing near the bottom of the recent range
ClimaxHeavy volume on the widest-range down bar in the lookback
TurnA bullish bar closing above the prior high, in the top of its own range

Plus: the climax must be recent, the fundamental score must clear, and the regime gate must pass.

StopSnapped at the climax bar — the structural low with an ATR pad. It does not move before the first target
TargetsThe short-average reclaim, then the long average — structure, not multiples
Time stopFifteen days below half an R
REV-RS · relative-strength survivor

In a correction most names fall. A survivor holds up, forms a higher low, and breaks its own resistance — these often lead the next leg.

recovery book ON · edge ON
AND relative strength slope positive
AND a higher low              recent low above the prior low
AND the pivot trend is up
AND the stock sits inside the correction band
AND close above the 20-bar prior high
AND volume above average
AND the sector in an acceptable stage
AND the fundamental score clears · regime gate passes
StopThe recent higher low, less padding
TrailChandelier ratchet with breakeven lock
Second targetThe 52-week high
REV-EARLY · early bird

The earliest possible recovery entry — and the highest risk of the three. It fires before strength has fully turned and before volume has confirmed.

recovery book ON · edge ON
AND trendline reclaim       close above the prior downtrend resistance
AND a higher low AND the pivot trend up
AND a compressed base       a narrow-range bar, or enough inside bars
AND a pivot break on volume
AND strength slope positive
AND sector stage · fundamental score · regime gate
StopThe lowest low inside the compression window, less padding
TrailChandelier ratchet

Because it is early, it should never be taken standalone — it needs context confirmation, and it is the edge most often left as a watch.

Recovery — Wyckoff

WYCKOFF · the accumulation base

Pure price and volume — no oscillators anywhere in it. It detects the three phases of an accumulation structure.

PhaseWhat must be present
B · the baseA long sideways range of contained volatility, following a substantial prior decline. Without the decline there is nothing to accumulate
C · the springA false breakdown just below the base low on weak volume, recovering back into the base within a few bars. The weak volume is the point — supply is exhausted
D · strengthA breakout above the minor resistance inside the base, on expanded volume
StopThe base low with an ATR pad
TargetsThe positional ladder — these are positional trades in character

Why this outranks the legacy recovery edges: a spring followed by a sign of strength describes a completed structure — supply exhausted, then demand proving itself. A bounce bar describes one day.

05Prerequisites — the hierarchy

Filters pass top to bottom before any edge is allowed to fire.

LevelRequirement
UniversalInside the backtest window · under the open-position cap · no existing position · fundamental gate (recovery only — bull edges bypass it)
BullMarket health — index above its long average with the medium above the long · weekly Stage 2 · base confirmed · alpha above floor · rotation leading or improving · the micro edge
RecoveryRegime gate — market corrected, or the stock inside its own correction band · sector in an acceptable stage · positive relative strength · the fundamental score
The asymmetry is deliberate

Bull edges bypass the fundamental filter; recovery edges cannot. A leader in an uptrend is being judged on demonstrated strength. A beaten-down name has no such evidence — the fundamentals are the only thing standing between “on sale” and “broken”.

The grid

Four quadrants — bull catalysts, recovery catalysts, rotation, and metadata. A blocked cell prints the exact name of the first failing gate rather than a generic cross.

The bracketed number is a HIT COUNT, not a score

A cell reading ✗ regime [8] does not mean eight criteria passed. The bracket is how many times this edge has fully fired across the chart's history. It is the most commonly misread element on the panel.

And one "block" that is not a block

When a shakeout and a strength push coincide, the panel promotes them to the combined cell, and the two standalone cells read shown as the combo. That is a promotion, not a failure. Always read the four Wyckoff cells together.

A display bug worth knowing as a class

An earlier version fired the Wyckoff triggers and took the trades — but never showed them. A live Wyckoff position read “wait” across every recovery row, hiding real exposure. The fix was display-only, with no change to any signal, entry, stop or target. A surface that acts but does not report is worse than one that does neither, because it looks calm.

The breakeven lock

Once the first target hits, every trailing stop is floored at the entry price. The runner cannot become a loss. This is also why the time stops do not fire on a position that has already reached breakeven — the runner is protected and can be held.

09Sizing

risk amount   = capital × risk %
stop distance = entry − initial stop
quantity      = min( risk amount / stop distance , max allocation / entry )

Then three modifiers, applied in order:

ModifierEffect
Regime discountOutside a bull market the risk percentage is cut, with a floor
Recovery discountRecovery trades always use the lower recovery risk — higher uncertainty, smaller bet
Kelly multiplier (bull only)All three of Stage 2, positive strength and volume → full. Two of three → standard. One → half
Volatility discountScales down with ATR as a share of price, with a floor so it cannot collapse the size

The two caps in the base formula matter more than the modifiers. Taking the minimum of risk-based and allocation-based quantity means a very tight stop cannot produce an oversized position — which is the failure mode a pure risk formula invites.

The daily loop, step by step

1 · Shortlist

Run the bull and recovery screeners. That is the candidate set — this script never replaces it.

2 · Pre-market

Load each shortlisted name. Read market health, stage and rotation first, then look for a firing signal. In that order — a green signal in the wrong regime is not a trade.

3 · Entry, by edge

Breakout · swing breakoutBuy-stop above the breakout level at the open
Accumulation · pullbackMarket at the open, or a limit at the short average
Gap and goValid on the gap day only — confirm volume before acting
Climax bottomThe next bar's open after the turn bar

4 · Management

  • Record the initial stop.
  • Log the catalyst label — it is what determines your trail type, and it is the field the ladder and the trail job both read.
  • Rest a limit at the first target; after it fills, the stop moves to breakeven automatically.
  • Hold the runner on the trail.

5 · Daily review

  • Is the trail still rising?
  • Stage 4 reached → close the positional trade immediately.
  • Close below the medium average → close the swing trade immediately.
  • Time stop hit under half an R and not yet at breakeven → close.
Running a backtest on it
  1. Enable the date-range filter and set the window.
  2. Open the strategy tester for the curve, the trade list and the profit factor.
  3. Turn off the book you are not testing — a pooled result across bull and recovery has repeatedly produced false conclusions here. Read it per family.
  4. Leave fast mode off. It skips checks, so its numbers are indicative at best.
